## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a premium, authoritative, and frictionless Arabic educational environment dedicated to global supply chains, international trade, and commercial importing from China. The brand voice balances institutional credibility with accessible, forward-looking trade logistics mastery.

### Emotional Goal
The user should feel confident, strategically empowered, and secure. Visual elements eliminate friction and ambiguity, presenting complex trade tariffs, container logistics, and supplier verification methods with absolute clarity and institutional prestige.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Corporate Editorial & RTL FinTech
- **Tonal Structure**: Clean corporate layering driven by crisp borders (`#E2E8F0`), pure white surface elevations, and cool ambient slate backdrops (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Accent Cadence**: Deep oceanic navy provides foundational trust, paired with luminous petroleum teal/cyan accents that indicate momentum, verified credentials, and actionable commerce insights.
- **RTL-Native Layout**: Built from the ground up for Arabic Right-to-Left (RTL) reading psychology, prioritizing right-anchored visual hierarchy, reversed horizontal progression lines, and context-tailored Arabic typographic metrics.

## Colors

The palette mirrors maritime shipping lanes, industrial trade hubs, and corporate executive education. High contrast and clean separation ensure strict accessibility across dense curriculum dashboards.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#0B192C`)**: Deep Maritime Navy. Anchors structural navigation, key headlines, deep cards, and authoritative institutional badges.
- **Secondary (`#00838F`)**: Deep Petroleum Teal. Applied to active states, verified trade tags, focus outlines, and primary progress metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#00B4D8`)**: Luminous Cyan. Used for high-impact micro-interactions, active lesson indicators, completion rings, and forward-pointing badges.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F8FAFC`)**: Canvas Mist. Prevents optic fatigue and provides subtle separation against pure white (`#FFFFFF`) content tiles.

### Semantic Tones & Accents
- **Border / Divider (`#E2E8F0`)**: Crisp, low-contrast boundary ensuring cards stay sharp without harsh visual breaks.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white base for all actionable and elevated card containers.
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep ink slate, avoiding pure black for softer legibility across extensive Arabic text.
- **Text Secondary (`#64748B`)**: Muted slate for metadata, lesson timestamps, and container specifications.
- **Success (`#059669`)**: Trade clearance green, customs verified status, and completed modules.
- **Warning / Alert (`#D97706`)**: Tariff calculation warning, port delays, and mandatory audit checkpoints.

## Typography

The design system employs **IBM Plex Sans** (with explicit pairing to **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** in production builds). This grotesque sans-serif delivers an engineered, technical precision that reflects maritime logistics, customs declarations, and commercial analytics while maintaining impeccable Arabic glyph clarity.

### Typographic Rules for Arabic (RTL)
- **Line Heights**: Arabic script requires approximately 20–25% greater vertical breathing room than Latin scripts to prevent diacritics and tall ascenders (such as *Alif* and *Lam*) from colliding with descenders (*Yaa*, *Raa*). All line heights are intentionally calibrated between `1.5x` and `1.65x`.
- **Weight Pairing**: Headlines rely on bold (`700`) and semibold (`600`) weights to produce clean visual anchors. Body text remains at regular (`400`) weight to maintain crisp counters and fluid readability.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: Quantitative data, Harmonized System (HS) codes, and pricing figures should utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) with clear Western or Eastern Arabic numeral consistency.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around strict Right-to-Left flow principles.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Single-column stack for courses, interactive shipment timelines, and student progress bars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns, `margin` (2rem / 32px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Dual-column layout for dashboard feeds and split-panel video lectures.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, max content width capped at `1280px` centered with auto-margins, `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px). Allows structured 3-column academic course grids or an 8:4 curriculum-to-sidebar workspace.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`)**: Used for chip tags, badge padding, and icon-to-label gaps (e.g., Arabic chevron to title).
- **Component Spacing (`space-md` to `space-lg`)**: Internal container padding for course cards, interactive tariff tools, and lesson list items.
- **Section Spacing (`space-xl`)**: Separates thematic modules, syllabus stages, and dashboard analytics groups.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through crisp borders combined with ambient, low-opacity cool-slate drop shadows rather than heavy skeuomorphic shading.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8FAFC`. The foundational backdrop for the entire platform.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Panels)**: `#FFFFFF` encased in a crisp border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Active Cards)**: `#FFFFFF` with primary border accent `1px solid #00838F33`. Shadow: `0px 10px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Flyout Menus)**: `#FFFFFF` with boundary border `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12), 0px 10px 10px -5px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Sticky Navigation / Player Controls)**: Surface tint `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a bottom border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled, modern curvature (`roundedness: 2` base = `0.5rem` / 8px) balancing corporate rigor with approachable learning software.

### Shape Roles
- **Buttons, Inputs & Dropdowns**: `0.5rem` (8px). Delivers tactile precision and clear touch targets.
- **Course & Module Cards**: `1rem` (16px, `rounded-lg`). Encloses educational media, syllabus trackers, and trading scenario widgets.
- **Hero Containers & Media Players**: `1.5rem` (24px, `rounded-xl`). Used for flagship masterclass overviews and video viewing shells.
- **Status Badges, Indicators & Pills**: `9999px` (Full pill). Applied to verified supplier tags, category filters, and live stage labels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Background `#0B192C`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `none`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover switches to `#00838F` with smooth `150ms ease` transition. In RTL, trailing directional icons appear on the left.
- **Secondary Button**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0B192C`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover state elevates to border `#00838F` and text `#00838F`.
- **Accent Action**: Background `#00838F`, text `#FFFFFF`, hover `#00B4D8`. Used exclusively for critical calls-to-action (e.g., "Start Import Masterclass").

### Badges & Category Chips
- **Academic Badges**: Semi-translucent surface tint (e.g., `#00838F15`), text `#00838F`, border `1px solid #00838F30`, radius `9999px`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Filter Chips**: Inactive state has `#FFFFFF` background with `#E2E8F0` border and `#64748B` label. Active state flips to `#0B192C` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards (Course & Knowledge Modules)
- Base background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `1rem` (`rounded-lg`), internal padding `1.5rem`.
- Features a top-aligned or right-aligned 16:9 media preview with an overlay pill for total hours and difficulty level.
- Footer section contains instructor attribution, verified customs icon, and a linear progress bar filled with `#00838F`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, radius `0.5rem`.
- Text aligns right by default. Prefix/suffix icons (such as currency `¥` or search magnifying glass) position correctly along the RTL axis.
- Focus state activates an outline halo: `border-color: #00838F`, `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 131, 143, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base dimension `20px x 20px`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `4px` for checkboxes and `50%` for radios.
- Checked state transitions immediately to `#00838F` with crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or center pip.

### Interactive Syllabus / Timeline
- Vertical progression line positioned on the right side of the list (`right: 24px`).
- Step nodes display completed states in `#00838F`, active modules highlighted in `#00B4D8` with subtle ping animation, and locked modules rendered in `#CBD5E1`.
